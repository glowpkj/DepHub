0.1.22 | Auto complete raid no longer picks islands by distance -- it walks them by number. The user checked the map with Dex and confirmed the islands are workspace.Map.RaidMap.RaidIsland1, RaidIsland2, RaidIsland3..., so the number IS the order, and "nearest" was the wrong idea: it could fly to an island that is close but out of sequence. Now it starts on island 1, stays while that island still has a live mob, advances to the next number only once it is clear, and when there is no next island AND no mob left alive it calls the round over: it stops acting and just waits, letting the game close the round on its own. It never restarts anything. Mobs are looked for in Workspace.Enemies and Workspace.Characters and inside the current island. Starting the toggle resets the sequence to island 1.
0.1.21 | Blox Fruits raid gets a second, narrower toggle: Auto complete raid. Where Auto raid buys the chip, flies to the tube and starts the raid, Auto complete finishes a raid that is already running -- and does nothing else. It reads the islands under workspace.Map.RaidMap, flies to the one nearest the player right now, lets the blade hit kill the mobs, then recalculates and moves to the next nearest, so it advances one island at a time instead of jumping around. No owner filter: it picks purely by distance, as the user specified. It holds off re-issuing the flight while already close (under 40 studs) so the character does not vibrate in place fighting the tween. The toggle turns the blade hit on when it reaches an island and turns it off again when switched off -- but only if the complete turned it on; a blade hit the player enabled stays enabled.
